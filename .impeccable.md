## Design Context

### Users
Thai SME owners and brand creators who need custom packaging (boxes) for their products. They are not designers — they want a simple, guided process to configure box dimensions, materials, and quantities, then get a quote and order. They use this tool on desktop primarily, during work hours, often comparing options.

### Brand Personality
**Professional, Clean, Trustworthy** — LumoPack is a packaging design studio that feels like a reliable business partner. It should inspire confidence that the final product will be high quality. The chatbot guides users step-by-step, so the UI must feel approachable without being childish.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Reference**: Canva / Figma — creative tool that's colorful yet clean, with clear hierarchy and intuitive interactions
- **Theme**: Light mode with purple as primary brand color, teal as secondary (chatbot accent)
- **Anti-references**: Generic dark-mode dashboards, overly corporate/boring enterprise tools, flashy neon gaming aesthetics
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans (display) + DM Sans (body) — already in place, good pairing
- **Key feeling**: "This company knows what they're doing" — clean whitespace, consistent spacing, subtle shadows, smooth transitions

### Design Principles
1. **Clarity over decoration** — Every element earns its place. No decorative noise. Information hierarchy is immediately obvious.
2. **Guided confidence** — The chatbot-driven flow should feel like talking to a knowledgeable salesperson. Progress is always visible, next steps are always clear.
3. **Consistent craft** — Same spacing scale, same border radii, same shadow levels throughout. Consistency builds trust.
4. **Responsive & fast** — Interactions feel instant. Loading states are graceful. Touch targets are generous.
5. **Approachable professionalism** — Warm enough to not feel corporate, polished enough to not feel amateur.
